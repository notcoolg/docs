## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="key-round" title="How do I reset my Economic ID password?">
    Open the [Economic ID login page](https://id.ecowestern.net/auth/login), select **Forgot password?**, and follow the reset instructions. If self-service recovery does not work, contact support. Recovery is not guaranteed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="arrow-right-left" title="Why was I redirected to Economic ID?">
    Supported EcoWestern products use Economic ID for sign-in. After you authenticate, Economic ID returns you to the product you were using.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="shield-alert" title="What should I do if I see an authentication error?">
    Record the error code, application name, approximate time, device, and browser. Share copied error URLs only through an approved support channel, and never share passwords, verification codes, backup codes, or session tokens.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Need more help?

Start with the relevant product guide and try the available self-service options. If they do not resolve your issue, submit a support ticket or contact the support team.
