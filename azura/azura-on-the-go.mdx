Azura works in mobile browsers with a responsive layout. There is nothing to install for web use.

## On a phone or tablet browser

- The sidebar becomes a slide-over. Tap the hamburger icon at the top to open it.
- The **Azura Artifacts** panel opens full-screen on small screens.
- Everything else — the Omnibox, uploads, voice input, slash commands — works the same as on desktop.

Voice input depends on your browser's speech recognition support. Chrome is the safest choice on Android.

## Sync across devices

Sign in with the same **Economic ID** account on each device and your conversations follow you:

| What | How it syncs |
|---|---|
| Conversation list (titles, pin/archive state, ordering) | In real time |
| Message content | Loads when you open the conversation |
| Artifacts, memory, usage | Not in real time |

Practical effects:

- A conversation you delete on one device disappears everywhere immediately.
- Opening the same conversation on two devices does not live-stream new messages between them — refresh to see the latest.

## About native apps

Azura has a native Android app in addition to the web app. For app-specific questions, check the app's store listing for current details. There are no iOS, Windows, or macOS apps.

## Offline

Azura requires an internet connection. There is no offline mode — if your device goes offline, a banner appears at the top reading "No internet connection. Messages may not be saved." Reconnect and click **"Retry"**. Messages sent while offline may be lost.
