**Long-term memory** lets Azura save short facts about you — preferences, projects, context — so future conversations are more personalized. Memory is on by default.

## Open the Memory settings

Choose any of these:

- Click the **Settings** gear in the header (tooltip **"Settings"**) and open the **"Memory"** tab.
- Use the `/settings` slash command.

The Settings modal is titled **"Settings"** with the eyebrow **"Personalize your workspace"**.

## Turn memory on or off

Use the **"Long-term Memory"** toggle:

- **On:** the status reads **"Active — Azura is learning."**
- **Off:** the status reads **"Disabled — Memories are sleeping."** Turning it off stops new memories from being saved.

## View and manage stored memories

The **"Stored Memories"** section shows a count badge with your usage against the current cap, plus:

- A search box (**"Search memories..."**).
- Sorting by **"Newest"** or **"Oldest"**.
- A **"Purge All"** button.

Memories are timestamped one-line facts. There is a cap on how many are kept — when full, the oldest memories are dropped first.

## Purge all memories

1. In Settings → **"Memory"**, click **"Purge All"**.
2. The confirmation dialog **"Purge All Memories"** asks: "Are you sure you want to delete all stored memories? This cannot be undone."
3. Confirm with **"Purge All"**.

You can also clear memory from chat: use the `/forget` command, review the **"Clear AI Memory"** dialog, and confirm with **"Clear Memory"**.

Purging cannot be undone.

## Privacy

Memories are private to your account. For the full picture of what Azura stores, see [Privacy and your data](/azura/privacy-and-your-data).
