This guide walks you through your first visit to Azura: signing in, finding your way around, and starting a conversation.

## Sign in

Azura uses **Economic ID** for sign-in. Open Azura and you are redirected to `id.ecowestern.net` to sign in, then returned to where you started. See [Sign in and your account](/azura/sign-in-and-account) for details.

## The home page

After signing in, you land on the home page:

- The heading reads **"Start with what is on your mind."**
- The **"New conversation"** button starts a fresh chat.
- Three quick-start cards each start a conversation:
  - **"Work through an idea"** — "Turn a rough thought into a clear plan."
  - **"Build something"** — "Create code, documents, or a working draft."
  - **"Understand a topic"** — "Get a useful explanation without the fluff."
- **"Continue where you left off"** lists your recent conversations, with a **"View all"** link that opens the sidebar.

## The interface at a glance

| Area | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sidebar** (left) | Your conversations, grouped by when you last used them, plus search and a **"New Chat"** button |
| **Chat area** (center) | The conversation feed |
| **Omnibox** (bottom) | The input box for messages, uploads, voice, and slash commands |
| **Header** (top) | Export, settings, theme toggle, and your profile |

On small screens the sidebar becomes a slide-over, opened with the hamburger icon.

## Start a conversation

Choose any of these:

1. On the home page, click **"New conversation"** or a quick-start card.
2. In the sidebar, click **"New Chat"**.
3. Press **Ctrl Shift N** (or **Cmd Shift N**).

A new conversation is titled **"New Conversation"** until you send your first message, after which Azura generates a title automatically.

## The welcome screen

New conversations show **"Where should we begin?"** with the subtext *"Ask a question, bring a draft, or describe the thing you want to make."* Below it, suggestion chips rotate over time. Click one to start, or just type your own message in the Omnibox.

## Send your first message

1. Click the Omnibox at the bottom of the screen.
2. Type your message.
3. Press **Enter** to send. Press **Shift Enter** for a new line without sending.

Azura responds as it works. See [Using the Omnibox](/azura/using-the-omnibox) for everything the input box can do.
