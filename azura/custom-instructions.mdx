**Custom Instructions** tell Azura how you want it to behave — your tone, format preferences, expertise level, or anything else. They are added on top of the default instructions and apply to all new messages.

## Set custom instructions

1. Click the **Settings** gear in the header (tooltip **"Settings"**), or use the `/settings` slash command.
2. Open the **"Instructions"** tab.
3. Type your instructions in the text area. A live character counter shows how much room you have left.
4. Click **"Save"**. The button flips to **"Saved"**.

When instructions are active, a note confirms: "Custom instructions are active and will apply to all new messages."

## Example instructions

The placeholder suggests examples like:

- "Always respond in a concise, bullet-point style."
- "You are an expert software engineer. Prefer TypeScript and functional patterns."
- "Never add disclaimers or caveats unless I ask."
- "Respond in British English."

## Good to know

- Custom instructions are stored **in your browser only**, not on the server. They will not follow you to another device or browser.
- There are no theme or model settings here. Theme uses the sun/moon toggle in the header; models use the in-chat selector above the Omnibox.
