**Azura — AI Assistant by EcoWestern** helps you think, make, and move forward. It answers questions, writes and reviews code, creates documents and interactive content, remembers what matters to you, and takes actions like searching the web or running Python during a conversation.

Azura runs in your browser at `azura.ecowestern.net`. There is nothing to install.

## Quick start

1. Open Azura in your browser.
2. Sign in with your **Economic ID** account. See [Sign in and your account](/azura/sign-in-and-account).
3. Click **New conversation**, or pick one of the quick-start cards on the home page.
4. Type your message in the Omnibox and press **Enter**.

## Good to know

- Azura is web-based. There is no offline mode.
- Your conversations are private to your account. See [Privacy and your data](/azura/privacy-and-your-data).
- Account details such as your name, email, and password are managed in the [Economic ID portal](https://id.ecowestern.net), not inside Azura.
