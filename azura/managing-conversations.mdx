Your conversations live in the sidebar, grouped under **"Pinned"**, **"Today"**, **"Yesterday"**, **"Last Week"**, **"Last Month"**, and **"Older"**. Hover a conversation to see its actions.

## Rename

1. Hover the conversation in the sidebar and click **Rename**.
2. Edit the title inline. Press **Enter** to save or **Escape** to cancel.

## Pin and archive

- **Pin** moves a conversation to the top under **"Pinned"**. Click **Unpin** to reverse it.
- **Archive** removes a conversation from the main list without deleting it. Click **Unarchive** to bring it back.

## Delete a conversation

1. Hover the conversation and click **Delete**.
2. The **"Delete Conversation"** dialog asks: `Are you sure you want to delete "{title}"? This cannot be undone.`
3. Confirm with **"Delete"**.

Deletion cannot be undone, and it syncs everywhere immediately — the conversation disappears on all your devices.

## Delete several conversations

1. Click the select-multiple toggle (tooltip **"Select multiple"**).
2. Select the conversations. A bar shows **"{n} selected"**.
3. Click **"Delete"**.
4. The **"Delete Conversations"** dialog asks: `Are you sure you want to delete {n} conversation(s)? This cannot be undone.`
5. Confirm with **"Delete All"**.

Click **"Exit bulk mode"** when you are done.

## Clear a conversation

Use the `/clear` slash command to delete the current conversation and return home. The **"Clear Conversation"** dialog warns "This cannot be undone." — confirm with **"Clear"**.

## Export a conversation

Use the export menu in the header (tooltip **"Export Conversation"**):

- **"Export as PDF"** — opens your browser's print dialog. Choose **"Save as PDF"** as the printer destination.
- **"Export as Markdown"** — downloads a `.md` file.
- **"Export as JSON"** — downloads a `.json` file.

## Search and pagination

- The sidebar search box (placeholder **"Search conversations..."**) filters your conversations. If nothing matches, it shows `No conversations match "{query}"`.
- The list loads 20 conversations at a time. Click **"Load more"** to see older ones.
- If you have no conversations, the sidebar shows **"No conversations yet"**.

## Syncing across devices

- Your **conversation list** — titles, pin and archive state, ordering — syncs in real time across devices signed into the same account.
- **Message content** loads when you open a conversation. It does not live-stream to your other devices.
- Artifacts, memory, and usage do not sync in real time.
