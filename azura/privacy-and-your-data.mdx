This page explains what Azura stores and how your data is protected.

## Your conversations are private to your account

Access is enforced at the database level — only you can view your conversations, messages, artifacts, and files.

## What Azura stores

- Your conversations and messages
- Generated artifacts, including their version history
- Files Azura works with in a conversation's private file workspace
- Your uploaded images and document text
- Usage counts
- Reasoning traces and tool activity — including URLs Azura read and Python code it ran — stored alongside messages. This is visible to you in the interface and private to your account.

## Memory

If **Long-term Memory** is on, Azura saves short facts about you — preferences, projects, context — so future conversations are more personalized. You are in control:

- View, search, and delete everything in **Settings → Memory** with **"Purge All"**. See [Memory](/azura/memory).
- Clear it from chat with the `/forget` command.
- Turn the toggle off to stop new memories.

## What stays in your browser

**Custom instructions and custom skills** are stored in your browser only, not on the server. They will not follow you to another device or browser.

## Deletion

Deleting a conversation removes it from your view everywhere — the deletion syncs immediately. Deletion is permanent from your perspective; there is no way to retrieve a deleted conversation.

Signing out clears locally stored settings from the browser.

## Model providers

Azura models run through EcoWestern's service. Some models are provided by third-party AI companies. Check the model selector for current provider branding — see [Choosing a model](/azura/choosing-a-model).

## Account security

Account details, passwords, and recovery are handled by **Economic ID** at [id.ecowestern.net](https://id.ecowestern.net). Never share your password, authentication codes, backup codes, or session tokens with anyone, including support staff.
