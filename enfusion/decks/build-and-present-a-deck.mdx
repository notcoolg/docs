**Envisiondeck** is the presentations app in Enfusion. Each slide is a 1280×720 canvas, and decks autosave about a second after you stop making changes.

## Create a deck

1. Go to **Envisiondeck** and click **"New deck"**.
2. On *"Start with a blank stage."*, name your deck. The placeholder is *"Untitled Deck"*.
3. Click **Create deck**.

Decks start blank. There are no deck templates.

## The deck editor

The editor header shows:

- The deck title, which you can edit inline.
- A save status: **"Saved"** or **"Saving..."**.
- A slide counter, such as "Slide 1 / 3".
- A **Background** color button.
- A zoom control from 25% to 300%. Click the percentage to reset the zoom to fit.
- The **Present** button.

The left sidebar shows numbered slide thumbnails. Hover a thumbnail to **Duplicate slide** or **Delete slide**. Delete is disabled while a deck has only one slide. Click **"Add slide"** to add more.

## Draw on a slide

Slides use the same canvas tools as inline canvases in notes:

- **Select** — pick and move elements.
- **Draw** — freehand drawing.
- Shapes — **Box**, **Circle**, **Star**, and **Arrow**.
- **Text** — click to place a text element, then double-click to edit it.
- **Color** — 10 preset swatches plus **Custom**.
- Font size from 10 to 72 for text elements.
- **Delete** — remove the selected element.

For alignment, snapping, and the grid, see [Use inline canvases](/enfusion/canvas/use-inline-canvases).

## Speaker notes

Open the resizable panel at the bottom of the editor and type notes for each slide. The placeholder is *"What do you want to say on this slide?"*. Notes appear below the slide while you present.

## Present a deck

1. Click **Present**.
2. The presentation opens fullscreen with a dark background.
3. Advance with a click, **→**, **Enter**, or **Space**.
4. Go back with **←**.
5. Press **Esc** or click **Exit · Esc** to stop.

The footer shows your position, such as "2 / 5 · Deck title", with **Previous**, **Next**, and **Exit · Esc** buttons.

## Limitations to know about

- Presentation is local-only on your screen. Decks cannot be shared, exported, or presented to remote audiences.
- There is no deck export in any format.
- Deleting a slide or deck is permanent. There is no trash or recycle bin.
