Enfusion uses **Economic ID**, the EcoWestern identity service, to sign you in. You sign in with your email address and password.

## Sign in to Enfusion

1. Go to the Enfusion URL in your browser.
2. If you are not signed in, Enfusion redirects you to Economic ID at `id.ecowestern.net`.
3. Sign in with your email address and password.
4. Economic ID returns you to the Enfusion page you started on.

After signing in, you land on **Home** with your recent work.

## Why was I redirected?

The redirect is expected. Enfusion does not store its own password. It sends you to Economic ID to confirm your identity, then brings you back. This is the same flow used by other EcoWestern products. For details, see [Understand redirects](/economic-id/connected-apps/single-sign-on).

## If your session expires

If your session ends while you work, the next action sends you to Economic ID to sign in again. Sign in, and you are returned to where you were. If you see a message such as "You need to be signed in to create a deck" or "You need to be signed in to create a note", refresh the page and sign in again.

## Troubleshooting sign-in

If you cannot sign in:

1. Confirm you are on the official domain: `id.ecowestern.net`.
2. Check your email address and password.
3. Use **Forgot password?** if you cannot remember your password.

For more help, see [Troubleshoot Economic ID access](/economic-id/troubleshooting/account-access). If self-service steps do not work, see [Contact support](/contact-support) to open a ticket.

Never share your password, authentication codes, backup codes, or session tokens with anyone, including support staff.
