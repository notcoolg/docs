When you sign in to Enfusion, you land on **Home**. This page introduces the main areas of the workspace.

## The sidebar

The sidebar on the left is your main navigation:

- **Home** — your greeting, recent work, apps, and pinned items.
- **Discover** — the template and app gallery.
- **Recents** — documents and notes you edited recently.
- **Starred** — a placeholder for a future feature. It has no page yet.
- **Shared with me** — documents other people shared with you.

Under **Apps**, you find the four Enfusion apps: **Envisiondocs**, **Excelerate** (coming soon), **Envisiondeck**, and **Envisionjot**.

## The top bar

The top bar contains:

- A search field with the placeholder *"Search your documents and notes..."*
- A **New** button that creates a new document.
- A theme toggle for light and dark mode.
- A **Ctrl K** button that opens the command palette.

## The Home page

Home shows:

- A greeting and a **Recent** list of your latest work.
- An **Apps** grid with shortcuts to each app.
- A **Pinned** section. If it is empty, you see *"Nothing pinned yet. Pin documents to see them here."*

If your account is managed by an **Economic Organization**, Home also shows a banner: "This account is managed by {org}".

## The command palette

Press **Ctrl K** (or **Cmd K**) from anywhere to open the command palette. From it you can:

- Jump to **Home**, **Discover**, **Recents**, or **Shared with me**.
- Create a new document or jot.
- Switch between light and dark mode.
- Run commands for the page you are on, such as renaming a deck or adding a tag.

Use the arrow keys to navigate and **Enter** to run a command. Press **Esc** to close the palette. The palette also fuzzy-searches commands as you type.
