Enfusion has a light and a dark theme.

## Switch the theme

Choose either method:

- Click the theme toggle in the top bar.
- Press **Ctrl K** (or **Cmd K**) and run **Switch to light mode** or **Switch to dark mode**.

## How the choice is remembered

Enfusion remembers your choice, so it stays the same between visits. If you have never picked a theme, Enfusion follows your system preference.

The theme preference is stored in a browser cookie that lasts about a year. If you clear cookies or use a different browser, the theme resets to your system preference until you choose again.
