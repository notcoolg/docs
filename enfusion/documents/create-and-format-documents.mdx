**Envisiondocs** is the documents app in Enfusion. It is a page-based word processor built around A4 sheets, so what you see is close to what prints.

## Create a document

1. Click **New** in the top bar, or go to **Discover** and pick an Envisiondocs template.
2. On the **"New document"** page, enter a **Title**. The placeholder is *"Untitled Document"*.
3. Click **Create**, or click **Create blank** to skip naming.
4. You land in the editor.

The title bar shows **Save** and **Share** buttons and a save indicator.

## Saving

Your document autosaves. The indicator shows **"Saved"** while changes are stored and **"Saving..."** while a save is in progress. Press **Ctrl S** (or **Cmd S**) to save immediately.

## The formatting toolbar

The toolbar above the page includes:

| Group | Controls |
|---|---|
| History | **Undo**, **Redo** |
| Text | Font size (8–72pt), **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Strikethrough** |
| Color | **Text color** (17-swatch palette), **Highlight** (same palette plus **None**) |
| Structure | **Heading 1**, **Heading 2**, **Heading 3**, **Bullet list**, **Ordered list**, **Task list**, **Blockquote** |
| Layout | **Align left**, **Align center**, **Align right**, line spacing (1.0 / 1.15 / 1.5 / 2.0) |
| Insert | **Horizontal rule**, **Page break**, **Insert link**, **Insert image**, **Insert table** (3×3 with a header row), **Code block** |
| Tools | **Find & replace**, word and page counter, **Outline** toggle, **Export** |

## Find and replace

1. Press **Ctrl F** (or **Cmd F**) to open **Find & replace**.
2. In **Find**, type the text to search for. The placeholder is *"Search for a word or phrase"*.
3. In **Replace with**, type the replacement. The placeholder is *"Replacement text"*.
4. Press **Enter** to search for the next match.
5. Click **Replace** to replace the current match, or **Replace all** to replace every match.
6. Press **Esc** to close.

## Outline

Click the **Outline** toggle to show a sidebar of your headings. Use it to jump between sections in long documents.
