## Before you begin

- You need a valid **email address** that you can access.
- You must be at least **13 years old**.
- You need the email address throughout setup so you can verify it.
- One Economic ID is enough for supported EcoWestern services.

---

## Creating your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your account details">
    Navigate to [id.ecowestern.net/auth/signup](https://id.ecowestern.net/auth/signup) or click **Sign Up** in any EcoWestern app when prompted.
    Enter your email address and choose a password. Use a unique password that you do not use elsewhere.

    <Tip>
      Use a unique password you don't use anywhere else, or generate one with a password manager.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your personal details">
    Enter your first name, last name, and date of birth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your country">
    Select your country from the available list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish your account">
    Enter your phone number when prompted and submit the form. Economic ID then sends you to email verification.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The current signup flow requires an email address, password, personal details, country, and phone number. If a field behaves differently in your region, follow the instructions shown on screen.
</Note>

## Verify your email

Open the message sent to your signup address and follow the verification link. If you do not receive it, see [I did not receive an email](/economic-id/getting-started/email-troubleshooting).

---

## Already have an account?

Go straight to [id.ecowestern.net/auth/login](https://id.ecowestern.net/auth/login) to sign in.

---

## Troubleshooting sign-up

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I didn't receive the verification email">
    1. Check your **spam / junk** folder.
    2. Make sure you entered your email address correctly.
    3. Wait a few minutes — delivery can occasionally be delayed.
    4. If the email still hasn't arrived after 10 minutes, return to the sign-up page and re-enter your details to trigger a new verification email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The verification link says it has expired">
    Use the resend option on the verification page. The exact expiration period is not currently documented, so request a new message if the link no longer works.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I get an error saying the email is already in use">
    Try signing in at [id.ecowestern.net/auth/login](https://id.ecowestern.net/auth/login). If you forgot your password, use **Forgot password**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The page shows a generic error">
    Try again in a supported browser. If self-service options do not resolve the problem, submit a support ticket. Account recovery is not guaranteed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
