## The simple version

Economic ID is one account for supported products in the EcoWestern EcoSystem. You can sign in with your email and password or use a passkey. An authenticator app may be required if you enable two-factor authentication.

---

## What happens when you sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a product or connected application">
    If you are not signed in, the product sends you to the official Economic ID sign-in page at `id.ecowestern.net`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You sign in with your Economic ID">
    Sign in with your email and password or a passkey. Complete two-factor verification if prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're signed in — everywhere">
    Supported products may recognize your active Economic ID session. Availability depends on the product, browser, and device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're sent back to where you started">
    Economic ID returns you to the product or connected application that started sign-in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## How long does my session last?

You may be asked to sign in again after a period of inactivity or when additional security verification is required. Session duration depends on the product and security configuration.

---

## What happens when I sign out?

Sign out from Economic ID or a product when you finish on a shared device. Sign-out behavior can vary between products; if you need to end access elsewhere, sign out of Economic ID directly.

---

## Your privacy

- Your password and authenticator codes should only be entered on `id.ecowestern.net`.
- Connected applications do not receive your Economic ID password.
- Do not share passwords, passkeys, verification codes, or recovery codes with another person or application.
