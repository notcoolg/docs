## Check these places first

1. Confirm that you entered the correct email address.
2. Check your spam, junk, promotions, and other filtered folders.
3. Search for **Economic ID** or `id.ecowestern.net`.
4. Wait a few minutes, then use the available **Resend** or reset option once.
5. Make sure your mailbox has room for new messages.

## Verification email

After signup, use the resend option on the email verification page. If the link no longer works, request a new message. Do not forward verification links to anyone else.

## Password reset email

On the Economic ID login page, select **Forgot password?**, enter your email address, and select **Send Reset Link**. If the link is invalid or expired, request another one.

If you no longer have access to your email address, try the available self-service recovery options first. You may [open a support ticket](/contact-support) if those options do not work, but account recovery is not guaranteed.

## Organization invitation email

An Economic Organization administrator may provide an invitation link if email delivery fails. Invitations expire after **7 days**, can be accepted only once, and must be accepted with the Economic ID email address used for the invitation.

## Still cannot find the message?

Submit a support ticket through the built-in support system. See [Contact support](/contact-support) for how to open a ticket and what to include. Include the email type, approximate time you requested it, and the address domain. Never include your password, authenticator code, backup code, or session token.
