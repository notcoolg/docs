## Your EcoWestern account

**Economic ID** is your EcoWestern account. Use it to sign in to supported EcoWestern products and services, manage your profile and security settings, and keep one identity across the **EcoWestern EcoSystem**.

Economic ID also provides **Economic ID Connect**, which lets connected applications authenticate you without receiving your Economic ID password.

## Key terms

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Economic ID** | Your EcoWestern account and identity service. |
| **Economic ID Connect** | The sign-in and authorization service used by connected applications. |
| **EcoWestern EcoSystem** | The collection of supported EcoWestern products and services. |
| **Economic Organization** | An organization managed through Economic ID. |
