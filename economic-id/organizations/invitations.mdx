## Before you begin

- Use the same email address that received the invitation.
- Make sure you can sign in to Economic ID.
- Accept the invitation within **7 days**.

## Accept an invitation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation">
    Select the invitation link in the email or use the fallback link provided by the organization administrator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to Economic ID">
    If prompted, sign in with the Economic ID email address that received the invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the invitation">
    Follow the on-screen instructions to accept the invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the organization">
    After a successful acceptance, Economic ID sends you to the organization's home page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invitation rules

- Invitations expire after 7 days.
- Each invitation can be accepted only once.
- You must use the invited email address.
- An account that already belongs to another Economic Organization cannot accept the invitation.
- Acceptance adds you as a regular **member** and clears a previous group assignment.

## If the invitation fails

If you signed in with the wrong account, sign out and reopen the invitation with the correct account. If the link is expired or has already been used, contact the organization administrator for help. The current product does not document self-service invitation resend or cancellation actions.

If you cannot access the organization after accepting, [open a support ticket](/contact-support) and contact your organization administrator. Never share your password or authentication codes.
