## Accessing your account dashboard

After signing in, go to your **Economic ID Home** at [id.ecowestern.net/id/home](https://id.ecowestern.net/id/home). This is the central hub for all account settings.

---

## Update your profile

Open [Economic ID home](https://id.ecowestern.net/id/home) and use the **Personal** area to review the profile fields available to your account. Depending on the current interface, you may be able to update your name, avatar, birthday, phone number, country, recovery email, language, theme, timezone, notifications, privacy, and accessibility settings.

<Note>
  The exact fields and verification steps can vary. Follow the instructions shown in Economic ID.
</Note>

---

## Changing your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Economic ID Home">
    Navigate to [id.ecowestern.net/id/home](https://id.ecowestern.net/id/home).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open password recovery">
    On the login page, select **Forgot password?**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a reset link">
    Enter your Economic ID email address and select **Send Reset Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new password">
    Open the link in the email and enter a new password. It must contain at least **8 characters**, and the confirmation must match.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a unique, randomly generated password managed by a password manager. Avoid reusing passwords across services.
</Tip>

---

## Forgot your password?

If you cannot sign in because you forgot your password:

1. Go to [id.ecowestern.net/auth/login](https://id.ecowestern.net/auth/login).
2. Click **Forgot password?** below the sign-in form.
3. Enter your registered email address and click **Send Reset Link**.
4. Check your email for the reset link and follow the instructions.

<Note>
  If the link is invalid or expired, request another reset link. If you no longer have access to your email, try self-service recovery first, then [open a support ticket](/contact-support) if needed. Recovery is not guaranteed.
</Note>

---

## Signing out

To sign out of your Economic ID:

- From **Economic ID Home**, click the **Sign Out** button.
- Signing out ends your Economic ID session in the current browser. Other products may handle their sessions differently.

To sign out on a device you no longer have access to, change your password. This revokes all sessions everywhere.

---

## Close your account

<Warning>
  Economic ID does not currently expose a visible self-service account deletion or closure action.
</Warning>

If you want to close your Economic ID, [open a support ticket](/contact-support) through the built-in support system. Include the reason for your request, but never include your password or authentication codes. Support may be able to help, but account closure is not guaranteed.
