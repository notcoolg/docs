## Single sign-on across EcoWestern products

Supported EcoWestern products can use your Economic ID session so you do not need to enter your credentials each time. Availability depends on the product, browser, and device.

If you are not signed in, a product may redirect you to `https://id.ecowestern.net/auth/login`. After you authenticate, Economic ID returns you to the product you were using.

## Connected applications

Economic ID Connect lets a connected application use Economic ID sign-in without handling your Economic ID password. Always check that credentials are entered on the official domain, `id.ecowestern.net`.

---

## What does this look like in practice?

You're browsing an EcoWestern app when you decide to switch to another. Instead of seeing a login page, you land directly on your dashboard — already signed in. Your Economic ID session travels with you across EcoWestern automatically.

---

## Why do I sometimes get sent to the Economic ID sign-in page?

There are a few common reasons:

- You have not signed in on this device or browser.
- Your session expired or additional verification is required.
- You signed out.
- Browser privacy settings, private browsing, or blocked cookies prevent the session from being recognized.

In all cases, just sign in normally and you'll be sent straight back to where you were trying to go.

---

## Signing out across all apps

Sign out of Economic ID when you finish on a shared device. Sign-out behavior may differ between connected applications.

This is the expected behavior and works this way by design, so you never get stuck partially signed in somewhere.

---

## What if I want to sign out of a device I no longer have?

If you have lost access to a device, sign in to Economic ID and review the available security options. If you cannot recover access, try self-service recovery first and then submit a support ticket. Recovery is not guaranteed.

---

## Is my account information shared between apps?

Connected applications may receive the account information needed to provide their service, but they do not receive your Economic ID password. Never share your password, authenticator code, passkey, or backup code.
