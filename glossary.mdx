# Glossary

Use this glossary to look up EcoWestern terminology used across the documentation. Terms are grouped by product area.

## EcoWestern EcoSystem

- **EcoWestern**: The company behind the EcoWestern EcoSystem.
- **EcoWestern EcoSystem**: The connected family of EcoWestern products — Economic ID, Azura, Enfusion, and eChat — that work together with a single account.
- **Economic ID**: Your EcoWestern identity. One sign-in that works across all EcoWestern products and connected apps. Use this exact name — never "Eco ID" or "EcoVerify".
- **Economic Organization**: An organization account in Economic ID that manages members, invitations, and access for a team, business, or community.

## Economic ID

- **Account**: Your personal Economic ID profile, including your sign-in details and security settings.
- **Connected app**: A non-EcoWestern product or service that you can sign in to using your Economic ID.
- **Single sign-on (SSO)**: Signing in once with your Economic ID to access multiple products without re-entering your password.
- **Recovery**: The self-service process for regaining access to your account if you lose your sign-in details. Try recovery first; if it fails, [contact support](/contact-support).

## Azura

- **Azura**: EcoWestern's AI assistant for conversation, research, and creating work.
- **Model**: The specific AI engine that powers an Azura conversation. You can choose between models depending on your task.
- **Omnibox**: The single input field where you type prompts, search, and start tasks in Azura.
- **Artifact**: A piece of content (like code, a document, or a diagram) that Azura creates alongside the chat so you can view, edit, and reuse it separately.
- **Memory**: What Azura remembers about you across conversations so responses stay relevant.
- **Custom instructions**: Standing directions you give Azura that apply to every conversation.
- **Skills**: Predefined capabilities you can enable so Azura can perform specific tasks.
- **Usage limits**: Caps on how much you can use Azura within a period of time.

## Enfusion

- **Enfusion**: EcoWestern's workspace for documents, notes, decks, and canvases.
- **Workspace**: Your Enfusion home base where all of your files live.
- **Envisiondocs**: The document editor inside Enfusion.
- **Envisionjot**: The note-taking tool inside Enfusion.
- **Envisiondeck**: The presentation tool inside Enfusion.
- **Canvas**: A freeform surface inside Enfusion documents where you can sketch, arrange, and connect ideas inline.
- **Template**: A prebuilt starting point for a document, deck, or note.
- **Shared link**: A URL that lets other people view or edit your Enfusion file, depending on the permissions you set.

## eChat

- **eChat**: EcoWestern's privacy-focused messaging and calling platform. It runs as a Progressive Web App on any device with a browser.
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: An app you install directly from your browser without an app store.
- **Direct message (DM)**: A private one-on-one conversation in eChat.
- **Group chat**: A conversation with multiple people. Group admins can add or remove members.
- **Channel**: A broadcast space in eChat for sending messages to a large audience, such as a community or announcement feed.
- **Reaction**: An emoji response (❤️ 👍 👎 😂 😮 😢) attached to a message.
- **Read receipt**: A confirmation that shows when someone has seen your message.
- **Voice note**: A recorded audio message you can send in a chat.
- **WebRTC**: The technology behind eChat calls, enabling direct audio and video connections between people.

## Getting help

If a term isn't listed here, try searching the docs, or [contact support](/contact-support). Never share passwords or recovery codes when asking for help.
